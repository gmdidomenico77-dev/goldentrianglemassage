A practice built where the rivers meet

Golden Triangle Massage takes its name from the neighborhood it serves: the heart of downtown Pittsburgh, held between three rivers.

The story

Pittsburgh has always known how to work hard. What downtown was missing was a place that took rest just as seriously. We founded Golden Triangle Massage on a simple conviction: that the people who keep this city running deserve an hour that is entirely theirs, in a room designed for nothing else.

We're a team, not a franchise. Each of our licensed therapists brings a different depth (some in clinical deep tissue, others in stone therapy, cupping, and the quieter arts) so that whatever your body is carrying, someone here knows how to lift it.

Our home at 320 Fort Duquesne Boulevard sits a short walk from the Point, where the Allegheny and Monongahela join the Ohio. The logo you'll see at our door (a skyline held in open hands) is a promise: this city, cared for.

What to expect

A first visit here is short on admin and long on the part you came for. It starts with a phone call rather than a form.

Call and tell us

A few minutes on the phone about what is aching and how much time you have. We recommend the session that fits and hold your place.

Arrive a little early

Time enough to settle, mention anything we should work around, and let the room start doing its work before the hour does.

Fifty unhurried minutes

Pressure and pacing adjusted as we go. Say something at any point: the conversation is part of the craft, not an interruption of it.

Leave with a plan

If what you are carrying needs more than one session, we will say so, and tell you when to come back rather than sell you a package.

A licensed therapist yourself? We would love to hear from you: give us a call at (412) 450-0880.

Come see for yourself.

The room is ready, the stones are warm, and your hour is waiting. One call is all it takes.
