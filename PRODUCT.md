# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Pittsburgh professionals, downtown residents, and visitors seeking a genuinely upscale massage experience — people who value skill *and* atmosphere and will pay for it. They arrive on the site curious or referred, often on a phone during a workday lull or an evening plan-making session. The job to be done: decide this is worth booking, then pick up the phone.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for Golden Triangle Massage, a team-based premium massage therapy practice at 320 Fort Duquesne Blvd in downtown Pittsburgh (the "Golden Triangle"). The site's job is to make a premium first impression and drive visitors to call. Success looks like a visitor feeling this is a treat worth booking — and dialing (412) 450-0880. **Booking is by phone only** (owner's decision, 2026-09-15); there is no online scheduler and the site must never imply one.

## Positioning

The premier downtown-Pittsburgh massage destination — a team of skilled therapists delivering a luxurious, restorative experience in the heart of the Golden Triangle.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Call (412) 450-0880** — a `tel:` link that shows the number as text, so it is useful on desktop as well as mobile. Present and dominant on every page.
- Secondary CTA: **Explore Services** — for visitors not yet ready to call.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *Pittsburgh's elevated, downtown massage destination.*
- Belief ladder: (1) this place is genuinely premium, not a chain → (2) the therapists are skilled professionals → (3) it's convenient — right downtown → (4) one call is all it takes.
- Proof on hand: placeholder testimonials for now; real quotes drop in later. Logo asset at `references/GTMLOGO2.png` (gold skyline-in-triangle cradled by hands, on black).

## Brand Personality

Understated, indulgent, confident. Quiet luxury first; trust and credibility close behind. The voice is calm and unhurried — never salesy, never clinical. Warmth carried by gold, ivory, and imagery, not by exclamation points.

## Anti-references

- Discount/chain massage aesthetics (Massage Envy).
- Busy Groupon-style layouts.
- The sage-green/cream spa look of `references/ref1.webp` — that file is a layout/spacing reference **only**, not a color or style reference.
- Generic AI-SaaS landing templates (hero-metric blocks, identical card grids, eyebrow labels on every section).

## Design Principles

1. **Understated luxury** — restraint reads as expensive; never loud or cluttered.
2. **The city is the story** — lean on the Golden Triangle / Pittsburgh identity as the differentiator.
3. **Confidence through calm** — generous negative space, unhurried pacing.
4. **Every element earns booking trust** — imagery, copy, and proof all point at the CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 and large text ≥3:1 against actual backgrounds, including over hero imagery (dimmed/scrimmed treatments). Gold is never a body-text color at small sizes; ivory carries body copy. Visible focus states, semantic landmarks, labeled forms, keyboard navigation, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation.
